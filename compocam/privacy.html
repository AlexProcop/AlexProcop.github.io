CompoCam privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

CompoCam – Compass Camera ("the app") is made by Oleksandr Prokopchuk, publishing as Colabox.NET ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on Android.

Everything stays on your device

  • The camera picture, compass, tilt and level readings are processed on your device to draw the overlay. Nothing is uploaded to us.
  • Viewshed, terrain profile and height results are calculated on your device.
  • Photos you take are saved only to your device's Pictures folder. We have no servers and no user accounts, so we cannot see them.
  • The app keeps a photo log on your device (time, position, bearing, tilt and note for each photo). It never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the photo log and settings. Photos you took stay in your gallery until you delete them.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your location while it is open, to show your coordinates on screen and to record the position of each photo in the on-device photo log. CompoCam Pro can also write the position into the photo's details (EXIF). The app does not use location in the background and never sends it to us.

Your position is determined by Android's location services. When the phone uses Wi-Fi or mobile networks to find it, Google may process that information as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Photos taken with CompoCam Pro may contain your location in their details. If you share such a photo, the location goes with it. You can turn off location for the app at any time in your Android settings.

Sharing and export

Photos and exported photo logs (CSV, KML, GPX) go only where you choose to send them, using Android's share menu.

Purchases

CompoCam Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether Pro was purchased on your account.

Map, terrain and building data

The map view, viewshed and terrain profile need data from online services. To get it, the app requests the map area you are looking at, which tells these services roughly where that area is (not who you are):

  • Google Maps draws the map. Google processes map requests as described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Terrain elevation tiles (Copernicus DEM and the AWS Terrain Tiles open data) are downloaded from Amazon Web Services for the area around the map.
  • Building outlines from OpenStreetMap are downloaded through the public Overpass API for the area around the map.

Downloaded data is cached on your device. These requests contain no account, name or advertising ID.

User manual

The manual is built into the app. When you open it, the app checks colabox.net for a newer version and, if there is one, shows it from the site. This is an ordinary web page request.

Ads in the free version

The free version shows a short video ad only when you choose to watch one to unlock the viewshed, terrain profile and height measurement tools for 60 minutes. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about the ad interactions and app usage needed to serve, measure and protect against fraud.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent screen before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy settings in the CompoCam Pro window. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

CompoCam Pro shows no ads, and the ads code does not start for Pro users. The app contains no analytics.

Permissions

  • Camera: to show the live picture and take photos.
  • Location (while the app is open): to show coordinates, record them in the photo log and, in Pro, add them to photos.
  • Internet: for the map, terrain and building data, the online manual, ads in the free version and checking your Pro purchase.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): to save photos to your gallery.
  • Vibration: for short feedback vibrations.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Ads are limited to family-friendly content.

Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The only data processing outside your device is by Google (location services, maps, ads and purchases) and by the map data services described above.
