Flexi Clock Widget privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Flexi Clock Widget (Seconds) ("the app") is made by Oleksandr Prokopchuk, publishing as Colabox.NET ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app on Android.

Everything stays on your device

  • The clock widget shows the time and date from your phone. Nothing about it is sent to us.
  • Your widget settings (colours, opacity, background) are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • The colour picker in the widget settings uses your wallpaper, or its main colours, on your device. The wallpaper is never uploaded or saved by the app.
  • We have no servers and no user accounts.

Ads

The widget settings screen shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. The clock widget itself shows no ads. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about ad interactions, app usage and diagnostics needed to serve and measure ads and to protect against fraud.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings.

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The app contains no analytics.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only to load ads on the settings screen.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The only data processing outside your device is by Google for ads, as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
